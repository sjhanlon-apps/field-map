⚡ The 4 rules

  • Tap Revolut everywhere you can.
  • Always pick VND / THB, never AUD, at ATMs and card machines.
  • Convert in Revolut Mon–Fri (1% fee on weekends).
  • Withdraw big and rarely, with your Revolut card.

💳 Revolut limits (Standard)

  • Free ATM: A$350 / 5 withdrawals a month, fee after.
  • Free FX: A$2,000 a month on weekdays.
  • Going over? Upgrade to Premium for the trip, cancel at home.

🇻🇳 Vietnam

  • ATM: VPBank. No machine fee, up to 10M VND (~A$570).
  • Avoid TPBank (~3.3%) and others (~50k VND / ~A$3).
  • Cash for street food, local massage, barber, tips.
  • AUD notes → gold shop in D1 (Hà Tâm, Lê Thánh Tôn) for the best rate.
  • Arrival: ~2M VND (~A$115) at SGN, then VPBank in town.

🇹🇭 Thailand

  • Every ATM charges 220–250 THB (~A$10–11).
  • So take out 20,000 THB (~A$900) at a time.
  • AUD notes → SuperRich for top rates.

🎒 Backup

  • A$500 in crisp, unmarked notes.
  • CBA card in the bag for emergencies only (~3% fees).
  • Card gone? Freeze it in the Revolut app.
